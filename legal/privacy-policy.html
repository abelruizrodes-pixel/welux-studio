Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026.

1. Data controller

The controller of your personal data is WeLux Digital Services S.à r.l - S. Registered office: Luxembourg. Email: info@weluxhub.com.

2. Data we process

We process (a) data you send us directly, such as your name, email address and the content of your message, when you write to us or request a digital invitation; (b) technical browsing data, such as your IP address, browser type, pages visited and the date and time of the visit; and (c) preferences stored in your browser (language and colour palette), stored locally on your device.

3. Purposes and legal basis

We use your data to answer your messages and provide the service you request (performance of pre-contractual measures taken at your request); to improve this site and its security (legitimate interest); and to remember your language and palette preferences (your consent, which you can withdraw by clearing your browser data).

4. Recipients

We do not sell or pass on your personal data. They are only shared with our technical providers: GitHub Pages (site hosting), Google Fonts (fonts) and the Tailwind CDN (stylesheets). If you request a digital invitation, your data is shared with ConCompás, which provides that service.

5. Transfers outside the European Economic Area

Some of our providers may process data outside the European Economic Area. In that case, the appropriate safeguards provided for by the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) apply, such as the European Commission’s standard contractual clauses.

6. Retention

We keep your messages for as long as needed to handle your request and for the applicable statutory periods. Preferences stored in your browser are kept until you delete them.

7. Your rights

You have the right to access, rectify or erase your data, restrict its processing, request its portability and object to its processing, and to withdraw your consent at any time. To exercise these rights, write to info@weluxhub.com. You can also lodge a complaint with the Commission nationale pour la protection des données (CNPD) of Luxembourg (cnpd.public.lu).

8. Security

We apply reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your personal data against loss, unauthorised access and disclosure.

9. Children

This site is not directed at children under 16 without the consent of their legal representatives.

10. Changes to this policy

We will update this policy when the way we process your data changes. The last update date appears at the top of the page.

11. Contact

For any question about this policy, write to info@weluxhub.com.
