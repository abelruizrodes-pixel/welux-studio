Cookie Policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026.

1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files that a website stores on your device to remember information about your visit, such as your language preferences. Browser local storage (localStorage) does a similar job: it stores data only on your device.

2. What do we use on this site?

This site does not use analytics, advertising or profiling cookies. Your preferences — your chosen language and colour palette — are stored only in your browser’s local storage (localStorage), not in cookies, and only to remember your choices when you come back.

3. Third-party services

The site loads resources from third parties to work: Google Fonts (fonts), the Tailwind CDN (stylesheets) and GitHub Pages (site hosting). Those services may process technical data, such as your IP address, to serve you the resources, in line with their own privacy policies.

4. How can I manage them?

You can delete the site’s data — cookies and local storage — from your browser settings, and block cookies there too. If you delete local storage, your language and palette return to their defaults.

5. Changes to this policy

We will update this policy if our use of cookies or local storage changes. The last update date appears at the top of the page.

6. Contact

For any question about this policy, write to info@weluxhub.com.
